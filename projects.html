Projects

Investigation of groundwater contamination in the villages surrounding UCIL, Kadapa (Project led by Dr. Ligy Phillip , Dr. B.S. Murthy , Dr. Venkatraman Srinivasan and members of Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board )

  • Performed an extensive survey of groundwater quality in different villages of Kadapa district surrounding Uranium Corporation of India Limited (UCIL) tailing pond.
  • Performed water quality analysis and produced a map of the entire region based on different water quality parameters.

Estimating evapotranspiration using satellite imagery and ancillary ground data (Project advisor: Dr. Balaji Narsimhan )

  • Estimated spatial variation of evapotranspiration using SEBEL model. The study area was the Tatra Mountains, located on the borders between Poland and Slovakia. LANDSAT 8 (OLI/TIRS) imagery was used for model analysis.
  • Produced GIS maps for land surface temperature, surface reflectance, NDVI, leaf area index, soil-adjusted vegetative index, longwave radiation, sensible heat and soil heat flux, latent heat flux, and evapotranspiration from the study area.

Optimal design of water distribution network using genetic algorithm (Project advisor: Dr. K.P. Sudheer))

  • Applied genetic algorithm technique for designing a water distribution network of multiple pipes having one standard outlet.
  • Quantified flux of water at each junction and the total amount of grey water at the outlet with the help of a MATLAB program utilizing genetic algorithm.

Modelling the effect of leaf water potential on stomatal conductance (Project advisor: Dr. Venkatraman Srinivasan)

  • Investigated the impact of leaf water potential on stomatal conductance using experimental methods of estimating leaf water potential by pressure chamber and leaf psychrometer (PSY1).
  • Produced plots for diurnal variation of leaf water potential at different soil moisture and weather conditions.

Flood inundation mapping for Adyar basin using HEC-RAS for 2005 and 2015 floods in Chennai (Project advisor: Dr. Balaji Narsimhan))

  • Produced flood inundation map for the Adyar basin for the flood of 2005 and 2015 using HEC-RAS. We used LANDSAT imagery for the production of maps using GIS.
