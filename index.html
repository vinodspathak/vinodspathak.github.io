Brief Bio

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil Engineering, at Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Madras, working at the intersection of water resources, plant hydraulics, and climate resilience. I joined IIT Madras for my M.Tech. and subsequently joined the PhD program in late 2019. My current research focuses on ecohydrology and functional–structural plant modeling, with an emphasis on understanding water uptake and transport within the soil–plant-atmosphere continuum (SPAC).

My research interests include, but are not limited to- Water-Energy-Food Nexus, and Large-Scale Agro-Ecohydrological Modeling . Specifically, I am highly interested in Climate Resilience in Plants, Precision Agriculture, Soil-Plant interactions, Remote Sensing, Hydro_ecological Modelling, and Physics-Informed Deep Learning.
