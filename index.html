Brief Bio

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil Engineering, at Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Madras, working at the intersection of soil, vegetation, and ecosystem processes. I joined IIT Madras as a graduate student, where I explored broader areas of hydraulics and water resources and conducted experimental investigations into soil–plant water relations, spanning physical and hydraulic properties, moisture dynamics, and plant hydraulic responses, including diurnal variations in soil and plant water potential and stomatal conductance under varying soil moisture and atmospheric conditions. I subsequently joined the PhD program, where my research evolved towards Ecohydrology and Functional–Structural Plant Modelling (FSPM), with an emphasis on understanding water uptake and transport within the soil–plant–atmosphere continuum (SPAC).

My research interests include, but are not limited to- Water-Energy-Food Nexus, and Large-Scale Agro-Ecohydrological Modeling . Specifically, I am highly interested in Climate Resilience in Plants, Precision Agriculture, Soil-Plant interactions, Remote Sensing, Hydro-Ecological Modelling, and Physics-Informed Deep Learning.
