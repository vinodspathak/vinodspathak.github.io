Research Activities

We recently developed a one-dimensional (1D) coupled soil-plant model to quantify fluxes in Soil-Plant-Atmosphere Continuum (SPAC). The model is open source and can be accessed here.

We developed a new plant hydraulic model (v-shoot) that predicts plant water uptake by explicitly representing individual leaves in a three-dimensional canopy architecture. The model details can be found here
